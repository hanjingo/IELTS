Q: Which do you prefer, e-books or paper books?

A: I appreciate both formats, but I have a slight preference for paper books. There's something uniquely satisfying about the tactile sensation of turning pages and the aroma of printed paper. However, I find e-books incredibly convenient for traveling and for accessing a vast library anytime and anywhere.

Q: When do you usually read online?

A: I typically read online when I need information quickly or when I'm researching specific topics. This often happens during breaks in my workday or in the evenings when I'm planning for the next day's tasks or exploring new interests.

Q: Will you read more online in the future?

A: Yes, I anticipate that I will read more online in the future. Digital platforms are continuously expanding, offering increasingly vast resources and reading materials. This convenience, coupled with interactive features like instant definitions and translations, makes online reading really appealing.

Q: Do you think paper books will disappear in the future?

A: I don't believe that paper books will disappear entirely in the future. While digital media continues to rise in popularity, many still cherish the physical aspect of paper books. They offer a nostalgic and sensory experience that e-books cannot fully replicate, ensuring their enduring presence in the reading landscape.
