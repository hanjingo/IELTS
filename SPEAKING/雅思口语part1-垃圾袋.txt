Q: What do you do when you see rubbish on the street?

A: Pick it up. If I can find a garbage can or recycling bin, I'll take it there. If I can't, I'll carry it with me until I can dispose of it at my house. I know it's yucky to carry it around, but it's worth it.

Q: Do you think it's important to keep the city clean?

A: Very! Our future generation relies on what we do to take care of our planet in the present. Furthermore, I believe we all have a social responsibility to do what we can to keep it as clean as possible. It is negative to our health, as well as how it looks aesthetically to have a dirty city.

Q: Do you think your city is clean or not?

A: It's a crying shame, but the city I'm living in is not as clean and tidy as it should be. Trash is strewn all over the place, from used water bottles to candy wrappers, and so on. I guess it's high time people became more environmentally conscious.

Q: Are you a tidy person?

A: Yes! I can't stand untidiness and dirt. My room has to be spotless. All my belongings have to be organized by color or by purpose. I won't have peace of mind until I clean and organize every single square inch of my room.
