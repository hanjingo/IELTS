Q: Do you want to be an artist?

A: Yes, I do harbor dreams of becoming an artist. The idea of expressing emotions and thoughts through strokes of paint or sculpture fascinates me. It's not just about creating beauty but also sparking conversations and challenging perspectives. Being an artist would allow me to weave my inner visions into tangible forms that can be shared with the world.

Q: Do you like modern art or traditional art?

A: I find myself drawn more to modern art because of its boldness and innovation. Modern art often challenges the norm and uses unconventional methods and materials, which I find incredibly stimulating. It's like a puzzle, sometimes perplexing yet profoundly meaningful once you start to unravel the artist's intent. However, I do appreciate the skill and historical significance of traditional art as well.
