Q: Did you enjoy singing when you were younger?

A: Certainly, I was fairly keen on singing, especially pop music, which was a fantastic way to entertain myself when I was younger. At that moment, boys were crazy about Jay Chou, and I even collected all of his albums.

Q: How often do you sing now?

A: Well, I would say at least twice a week. As I have to work during weekdays, I usually sing on weekends, especially when I hang out with my friends in KTV.

Q: Do you have a favorite song you like listening to?

A: My favorite single song is My Heart Will Go On, which was a hit when it was released. It was sung by one of the famous female singers from Canada--Celine Dion. I was deeply attracted by her clear and sexy voice. Dion's music frequently contains elements of love, which is so melody.
