Q: Which instrument do you like listening to most?

A: I enjoy listening to the piano most. The reason is simple: it's pleasing to the ear. Besides, the piano is a versatile instrument. In terms of the power of expressiveness, no other instruments can match it.

Q: Have you ever learned to play a musical instrument?

A: Yes, I have. I joined a violin class when I was in the primary school. It took me a bit of time to master the techniques. There were quite a few squeaks at first. I'd say it was really a torture to people around me: my parents and the tutor, but they gave me lots of encouragement. Although I'm still an amateur, I get great pleasure from playing it.

Q: Do you think children should learn to play a musical instrument at school?

A: Yes, that's for sure. For parents and teachers, having children learn to play an instrument helps identify their talent for music at an early age. For children, learning to play an instrument allows them to develop creativity and express themselves. I personally believe that if there's one thing you should learn in your lifetime, it is how to play an instrument.

Q: How easy would it be to learn to play an instrument without a teacher?

A: Personally, I think it would be quite difficult to learn without a teacher. Music shops do sell books that claim to teach you to play, but I think they may work for a few clever people, while in most cases you'll find it impossible. You cannot master the subtle and complex techniques without the instruction of a real live teacher.
