Q: What is your date of birth?

A: Well, I was born on September 8th 1993. So every year, I celebrate my birthday on the 8th of September.

Q: When was the last time you celebrated your birthday?

A: I celebrated my birthday last year. My family do not miss the opportunity to celebrate any of our birthdays! It was mostly a family celebration where a few of my relatives and friends were present.

Q: Describe a birthday party you have attended?

A: I went to the birthday party of one of my closest buddies a few months ago. Some other intimate friends were also invited to the party. A beautiful birthday cake was made marking the day. The dishes were delicious too. I gave a nice-looking T-shirt and a small perfume bottle to my friend, and he was happy with the gifts. He also received many other on this day. We all enjoyed the party at the rooftop of this residence. It was a memorable event indeed for all of us.

Q: Do you celebrate your family members' birthday?

A: Ummm... actually, my parents love to celebrate our birthdays and I love to have those happy events. I have two sisters and all of our birthdays are celebrated - sometimes at home and sometimes at restaurants. However, my parent avoid celebrating their own birthdays by all means. It was a perplexing issue for me as a child but now I realise why many adults do not like to celebrate their own birthdays.
