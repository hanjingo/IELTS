Q: When did you start using the internet?

A: I started using the internet at a relatively young age, around when I was ten years old. It began with educational games and gradually expanded to researching for school projects and exploring various interests.

Q: How often do you go online?

A: I go online daily. The Internet is an integral part of my routine, used for both professional purposes and personal enrichment. Whether it's for checking emails, catching up on news, or engaging with social media, it's rare for a day to pass without me connecting to the internet.

Q: How does the internet influence people?

A: The internet has profound influence on people by providing immediate access to information and facilitating global communication. It shapes opinions through the rapid dissemination of news and enables social interactions that were not possible before. However, it also presents challenges, such as information overload and the spread of misinformation.

Q: Do you think you spend too much time online?

A: At times, I do feel that I spend more time online than necessary. While the internet is a powerful tool for connectivity and learning, it is also easy to become distracted by less productive activities. I am continuously working on balancing my online activities with offline experiences to maintain a healthy digital lifestyle.
