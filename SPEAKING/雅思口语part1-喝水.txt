Q: How often do you drink water?

A: Well, every day of course! Staying hydrated (providing water for someone to keep them healthy) is a priority for me since I easily get a headache if I don't, not to mention my weight loss goals. Drinking enough water is of paramount importance for health.

Q: What kind of water do you like to drink?

A: All different kinds; I'm not too picky! I either drink tap water that I put in a Brita filter, or bottled water from time to time. I usually put water in my refillable water bottle and carry it around me with me all day.

Q: Do you drink bottled water or water from water machines?

A: Bottled water. Since the water in my country is drinkable, I usually opt for that with a filter to improve the flavor. If this isn't available to me, I buy a bottle of water, although I prefer not to because of my environmental concerns.

Q: Do you like to play in the water?

A: It's just so much fun. I often say that one of the best feelings in life is to just jump in a cold pool on a super-hot day. It's one of my favorite ways to unwind on a summer afternoon.
