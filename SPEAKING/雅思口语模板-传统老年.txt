Aging.

Aging is an unavoidable natural process.

China has entered the aging society period.

Generation.

Different generations shall be more understanding of each other.

Many old people today are even more open-minded than the young.

Old people are more conservative about what to wear.

Old people sometimes behave like children.

The rich life experiences and wisdom of elderly people are a treasure.

History.

I think historical dates play an imperative role in our daily lives.

Only by knowing about the history of your country, you understand why you are here and what you are going to be in the future.

There are a variety of ways to learn about history.

Retirement.

The retirement age for regular laborers should be 55 for women and 60 for men.

Tradition.

Now, some young people have no interest in traditional art at all.

Old people pass down traditions to the younger generation.

Respecting old people is a long-standing tradition in China.

The government should give much support to the protection of the traditional arts.

To protect traditional Chinese art, we should nurture children's interest in them.

Traditional art is a national treasure.

We should pass on the cultural heritage to the coming generation.
