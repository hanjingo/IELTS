The technique I had to enhance my learning experience was a technique called space repetition, specifically for my vast syllabus of the Civil Services Examination.

I came across this concept while reading a book on effective study habits.

Space repetition is a technique that involves reviewing information on increasing intervals, allowing for better retention and long-term memory.

I first though about this concept in my final year of university when I was preparing for my Civil Services Examination; which is a highly competitive and comprehensive exam that assesses the candidates' knowledge about various topics like history geography and current affairs.

I realized that cramming information the night before the exam was it enough to grasp and retain the whole syllabus.

By implementing space repetition, I create a study schedule that incorporates regular reviews of key concepts and facts of the study subject.

Instead of focusing solely on recent topics, I review previously covered material and gradually increase the gap between each review session.

What encouraged me to think about this idea was the vast syllabus of the Indian Civil Services Examination and the need to develop a more effective learning strategy.

I was inspired by research and success stories of previous candidates who have utilized space repetition for their preparation and have achieved good results in their exam.

These tools will help me prioritize and monitor the topics, set up customized review intervals, and track my progress.

By integrating technology into my study routine, I believe I can optimize the implementation of space repetition and make my learning even more efficient and targeted.
