Q: Is it difficult for you to stay focused on something?

A: Yes, I've got a poor concentration span. Well, a lot of the time, anyway. There are times when I can focus really well - when I've had a good night's sleep or when I am feeling calm and confident. But if I am stressed or nervous, I find it hard to concentrate on things, and I am easily distracted.

Q: What do you do to help you concentrate?

A: I put on noise-canceling headphones - you know, you know, that kind of headphones that play music but do not allow other external sounds to enter your ears! I put on light classical music, and I make sure I shut the door and I tell my friends and family that I need to work in peace.

Q: What may distract you when you are trying to stay focused?

A: When I am trying to stay focused, I can get easily distracted by many things. Firstly, my own thoughts start to wander off, and I suddenly realize I am staring out of the window, thinking about something completely different and not focusing on my work. Sometimes, if I hear music or my parents or friends talking outside the room, I start to lose my concentration. Things like that.

Q: When do you need to be focused?

A: Usually, I need to concentrate and be focused when I am doing work tasks. Some people can study when there is a lot of noise around them, but I can't, I have to be in complete silence. I must be in a place where there is no music, no household noise, no people talking... total silence. Maybe I am not good at concentrating, and I get distracted too easily... but that's just the way I am. It's my personality.
