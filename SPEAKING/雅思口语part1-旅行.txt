Q: Do you enjoy traveling?

A: Actually, traveling is definitely my cup of tea. It is a perfect way for me to get relax and enlarge my horizons by getting to know some fascinating exotic cultures. For example, I was totally stunned when I saw the elephants walking in the streets of Thailand.

Q: Have you done much traveling?

A: Sure, I have traveled to tons of places so far. It is no exaggeration to say I can't live without traveling now. It makes my life become meaningful. Every summer vacation I travel by myself, I have found that traveling is the best way to keep away from trivial things in daily life. By the way, I treat traveling as the finest way of mental therapy.

Q: Do you think it is better to travel alone or with other people?

A: Well, from my point of view, as a young man I would probably choose to travel alone. It is obvious that traveling alone can give me more freedom, I mean I won't be affected by others, will. And more importantly, my trip can be more personalized, and I can design my own itinerary based on my preferences. For example, the last time I went to Hong Kong I skipped some "must-see" spots, such as Ocean Park and Disneyland, but went to Lan Kwai Fong which is my desirable place.

Q: Where would you like to travel in the future?

A: Well, if I had enough time and money, I hope to travel to Hawaii. Actually, I am a big fan of the history of the Second World War. I am so eager to visit the USS Arizona memorial and the battleship Missouri which are the most well-known Second World War attractions.
