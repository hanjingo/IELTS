Q: What types of music do you like to listen to?

A: As a middle-aged person who is nearly occupied by daily work, frequently listening to music in my spare time seems rather luxurious for me. And I only get used to listening to pop music. In fact, the real reason is that the radio channel in my car often plays pop music, and now I'm into it somewhat.

Q: At what times of day do you like to listen to music?

A: My times of day to listen to music are quite regular, and it often happens at noon and at night. The principal reason is that I have about two hours' lunch time which is rather long for me. Besides, listening to light music before I go to sleep makes me comfortable.

Q: Did you learn to play a musical instrument when you were a child?

A: Yes, I began to learn to play a musical instrument when I was about three years old. The first musical instrument that I played was the piano. However, at that time I didn't know why I needed to do it because I was just told by my parents that I should learn it without any explanation.

Q: Do you think all children should learn to play a musical instrument?

A: Yes, I suppose so. Perhaps some people might disagree with me in terms of this point, but my reason is that learning a musical instrument is something pleasant to all children no matter whether they can play it well. From my point of view, being happy is an indispensable part of childhood life.
