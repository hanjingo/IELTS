Q: What games are popular in your country?

A: It varies. For our young generation, there is only one name for games that we are crazy about, which is the computer games, either single-user version or concurrent version. For the elder generation, like my grandpa, they have a lot of folk games to play, such as shuttlecock, diabolo, and jumping rope. As for the reason why such kind of situation appears, personally, I think it has much to do with the changes of times and technology.

Q: Do you play any games?

A: Before I give you my answer, I have to remind you that I'm a super game player. Of course, I play games and played a great number of diversified ones. When I moved on the dancing blanket, I enjoyed the feeling of dancing to the music. When I played the Megga Jump, I couldn't help overcoming all the obstacles to win. All these games not only help me relax but also benefit me with the ambition to succeed.

Q: How do people learn to play games in your country?

A: Traditional games were quite simple and easy to learn. With the development of society, one huge difference nowadays is that we are obsessed with high-tech computer games. Referring to how to learn it, it's pretty convenient to find breakthroughs on the Internet for players. They are experts in Counter-Strike and WarCraft without devoting too much time to learning the game skills.

Q: Do you think it's important for people to play games?

A: Definitely yes. Games to people are what condiments to diet. Without games, our life will lose soe interests and become dull. Imagine it, when you go through a tough day surrounded by tired work, when you are out of the mood, and even when you don't know how to kill time, why not take the game as a weapon to re-energise yourself?
