Q: How many languages do you speak?

A: I speak fluent Mandarin and Hunan dialect, and I would like to believe I speak English as well.

Q: Why is it important to speak a second language?

A: I believe that speaking many seconds or many languages helps you to communicate with more people and at the same time to keep your brain sharp.

Q: What are the most common languages in your country?

A: Mandarin is the really common language in China, although these days English is becoming very popular as well, as this is an international language of tourism and commerce.

Q: Where do you hear people speak in another language most often?

A: The place which is coming to my mind would be the famous Guangzhou City. This is where a lot of local residents and international merchants go, and if you're there, you can hear people speaking Cantonese, Mandarin, and English.

Q: When have you needed to speak in another language?

A: I use English at my work, but I use a little Cantonese sometimes to speak with local residents while going shopping.

Q: If you could choose one more language to learn, what would it be?

A: Given the chance, I would learn Japanese, because I always wanted to visit Japanese, and that would be really good if I could speak in their native language.
