Q: Do people in your country wear sunglasses?

A: Absolutely. But since South China has a rainy climate, they aren't as needed as in other places. Still, people like to wear them as a fashion statement or as an accessory to their outfits.

Q: Do you like to wear sunglasses?

A: Of course, especially when I feel that I look tired. Moreover, I like that they can tie a whole outfit together. Moreover, when I drive on sunny days, sunglasses are a must.

Q: Do you often wear sunglasses?

A: Not too often actually because of the climate here. I'd say I have to wear them a couple of times a month on the rare occasion that the sun comes out. As I mentioned before, I feel I need them most when I drive on sunny days or when I go on holiday to the beach.

Q: Where do you buy sunglasses?

A: I tend not to splurge on sunglasses since I have the tendency to lose or break them, so I usually just buy knock-off brands from street vendors. I've bought and lost several pairs of knock-off Ray-Bans, which were just fine for me. I feel that the shape of them suits my round face shape.
