Q: What types of photos do you like taking?

A: Well, I'm keen on taking photos of people who I know, especially people who I meet every day. I think taking photos of them is a way for me to record our life. When we are old, seeing photos in the albums that we took many years ago must be something interesting. Additionally, I can know who I used to spend much time with and what our life was like before.

Q: What do you do with the photos you take?

A: I used to print out the photos that I took, and then of course put them in my albums. However, this is so old-fashioned nowadays, In recent years, I enjoy storing photos on my laptop or smartphone, and if there are some quite interesting or beautiful photos, I will upload them to my social networking site accounts to share with my friends.

Q: When you visit other places, do you take photos or buy postcards?

A: Undoubtedly, I will buy postcards whenever I see them. Pictures on postcards are often taken by professional photographers, and that is why pictures are breathtakingly beautiful. By the way, postcards are a kind of collection that can help people to keep lots of wonderful memories that they had while traveling. Definitely, if you want, postcards can also be used as gifts for friends or relatives.

Q: Do you like people taking photos of you?

A: I dislike people taking photos of me, I usually feel nervous when standing in front of the camera.
