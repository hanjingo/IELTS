Q: Have you ever been in very cold weather?

A: I live in the Guangdong province which is located in the southern of China, and the weather is hot and humid. However, I did live in very cold weather for some time when I visited my hometown, Hunan province. In order to live with my grandmother and grandfather for spring festival. Of course, I loved the snow there, but it was just way too cold for me probably because I am living in a different kind of weather. 

Q: How often is the weather cold where you come from?

A: Where I live in Guangzhou, the weather remains cold for about 2 to 3 months from November to February, sometimes stretching till the month of March. However, our winter period can be really dry where temperatures can vary from 10 degrees Celsius to 25 degrees Celsius.

Q: Are some parts of your country colder than others?

A: China is a very big country, and its temperatures can vary extremely from the North to the South as well as valley to valley, depending on the elevation. For example, the northern part of China, bordering Russia, can be extremely cold and can be covered with snow because it sits on a rather high altitude while southern China mostly experiences a sub-tropical climate, because of its lower altitude where winter typically lasts from November to March.

Q: Would you prefer to live in a hot place or a cold place?

A: Sometimes, I feel cold even during the summertime (probably because of my low body temperature, I suppose). Therefore, the hotter the weather is, the better it so for me. Besides, I feel that putting on too many clothes every time I go out and then taking them off after coming back home, is just a terrible waste of time. Then again, I could be wrong too about this "whole practice" of changing clothes!
