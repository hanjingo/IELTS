Q: Did you like your deskmates in primary school?

A: Yes, I liked my deskmates in primary school. We shared many interests and supported each other in our studies and during playtime. The friendships I formed at my desk were foundational, teaching me valuable lessons about cooperation and mutual respect.

Q: Can students choose their desk mates in your country?

A: In my country, students typically cannot choose their desk mates. Teachers usually assign seating arrangements to encourage diversity in interactions and to help students develop the ability to work and get along with different individuals. This approach can foster inclusivity and teamwork.

Q: Is it good for children to play with their classmates?

A: Absolutely, it is beneficial for children to play with their classmates. Playtime not only serves as a break from academic activities but also plays a critical role in social development. It helps children learn teamwork, empathy, and problem-solving skills. Additionally, playing together strengthens friendships and enhances classroom harmony.
