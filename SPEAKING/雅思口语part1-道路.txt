Q: Are the roads in the area where you live busy?

A: Quite busy, especially during rush hours. The main road near my place is always packed with cars and buses. But the side streets are quieter, which is nice for walking or cycling when you want to avoid the hustle and bustle.

Q: How do people cross the road in the city where you live?

A: We have a mix of options. There are zebra crossings and traffic lights at major intersections. In busy areas, there are also overhead pedestrian bridges. Some people jaywalk, though it's risky and not recommended!

Q: How is the condition of the roads in your city?

A: Overall, not bad, but there's definitely room for improvement. Most major roads are in decent shape, though you'll find the occasional pothole. The real issue is with some of the older residential streets - they can be quite bumpy. During rush hour, the main arteries get pretty congested, which isn't great for the road surface.

Q: Do you think the roads in your city need improvement?

A: Absolutely. While they're functional, there's a lot we could do better. I'd love to see more cycling lanes to encourage eco-friendly transport. Improving traffic flow at major intersections would be a big help too. Oh, and don't get me started on parking - we desperately need better parking solutions there. It's not just about fixing what we have, but rethinking our raod systems for the future.
