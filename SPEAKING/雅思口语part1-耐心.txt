Q: Do you have patience?

A: Not at all! I'm quite an impatient person. I often feel on edge, especially when I'm behind the wheel or waiting in line. Moreover, I like immediate gratification, so I tend to lose my patience when I cannot obtain this. This is one of my biggest downfalls.

Q: Do most people in your country have patience?

A: Not really. Since I come from a country of people who are used to having all conveniences at their fingertips, they often become impatient when this doesn't happen. Furthermore, people spend a lot of time commuting to their offices in the mornings, which tends to leave them feeling antsy as well as angry at other drivers.

Q: Have you ever lost patience?

A: Who hasn't? There have been several instances. I once became quite impatient while I was waiting in a convenience store, not to mention with people cutting the line repeatedly, and I just left my items there idly and stormed out of the store.

Q: When do you need patience the most?

A: That's a difficult question. I think having patience in general is quite a virtue. I think it's good to have it with your life in general. When you have this, I imagine your life goes more at ease from step to step. Moreover, I think it's important to have it with your children so that they aren't raised in an angry environment, and so they can develop it later on in life.
