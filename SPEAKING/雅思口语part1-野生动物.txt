Q: Are there any wild animals in your country?

A: Yes, my country is quite big, and there are icy regions, tropical regions and desert regions, so we have a wide variety of wild animals of all sorts of different kinds. I believe we also have animals like tigers, in certain regions, but they're an endangered species.

Q: Have you ever been to a zoo or a safari park?

A: I have yes. I've been to the kind of zoo where you see a lot of animals in different cages and zones, and I've also been to a drive-through safari where you go through a large area in your car and the animals come right up to your windows sometimes - rhinos, giraffes and so forth.

Q: What is the animal you would like to see in the wild?

A: If I could go on a really wild safari or adventure holiday, I'd go to Africa, to the Serengeti plains and I'd like to see giraffes. I love giraffes, I think they're so special and unique. I'd love to have the chance to study them up close and learn more about them in their natural habitat.

Q: Are there TV programs about wild animals in your country?

A: Yes, there are plenty of wildlife documentaries aimed at both adults and children. They are fairly popular. People like to learn about animals and their habits and lifestyles.
