Q: How often do you use maps on your phone?

A: I use maps on my phone quite frequently, especially when I'm traveling to unfamiliar places. While I consider myself decent at using maps, technology has made navigation much more accessible. I don't have physical maps at home, but I do have digital maps readily available on my devices.

Q: Are you good at using maps?

A: I'm reasonably proficient at using maps, especially digital ones on my phone. However, I do occasionally make wrong turns or get momentarily lost, which can be a bit frustrating.

Q: Are there any maps in your home?

A: I don't keep physical maps in my home, as digital maps are more convenient and up-to-date. However, I do have a collection of maps from places I've visited as souvenirs, neatly stored away as mementos of my travels.
