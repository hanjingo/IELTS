Q: What kinds of things make you laugh?

A: Different people from different countries have a different sense of humor. I tend to laugh at situation humor rather than jokes. I laugh when the pompous get themselves into situations that they have created and cannot get out. I don't find it funny when innocent people are humiliated or made figures of fun.

Q: Do you like making other people laugh?

A: I like to make people laugh and I tend to do it by describing situations where funny things happened as a result of my innocence or naivety or when I got the better of the pompous or self-righteous. I think it is very important to be able to laugh at yourself and not take yourself too seriously. Making people laugh in this way is a way of letting people share your life.

Q: Do you think it's important for people to laugh?

A: I think laughter is very important because it is a way of coming to terms with a variety of situations. It is also a way of releasing inner tension. Statistics have shown that people who are able to laugh at themselves cope with life much better. Laughter can also help to put worries into perspective.

Q: Is laughing the same as feeling happy, do you think?

A: Laughter is often a short-term response to a situation, whereas happiness is a more long-term thing. One can laugh at a situation for a few moments, but at the same time, one can be very unhappy. One can also be happy without needing to laugh. A son or daughter has gained a good job in a foreign country. You are happy because they have got what they were looking for and you share that happiness, yet, at the same time laughter here would be out of place--it would add nothing. You might be sad that they will be living far from you.
