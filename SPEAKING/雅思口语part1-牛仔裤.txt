Q: Do you wear jeans?

A: Yes, I have many pairs of jeans in my wardrobe because they are very durable. Like the one I am wearing, it hasn't yet stretched out, although I washed it many times.

Q: How often do you wear jeans?

A: I wear them on a daily basis. I mean, I am sick of formal attire like shirts or trousers that I have to wear in my workplace. That's why I always choose a pair of jeans, mixed with a short-sleeved T-shirt and a denim jacket when I go out.

Q: Do people in your country like wearing jeans, why?

A: Yes, jeans are the clothing of choice for the majority of Chinese people, especially the youth, as they are very easy to mix and match. In the past 2 decades, people in my country loved wearing flare jeans, but skinny jeans are now on trend.
