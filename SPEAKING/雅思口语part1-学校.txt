Q: Did you go to a secondary or high school near where you lived?

A: Yes, my high school was only two blocks away from where I was living at that time. Hereby, I did not need to spend as much time as some of my classmates did by taking public transport. Students living pretty far away from our high school had to get up extremely early every morning. While they were standing in a huge crowd on the subway, I was probably still dreaming.

Q: Where is your school?

A: My school is located in the heart of Changsha, the capital of Hunan province. It's situated in a bustling area with convenient transportation, surrounded by many shops, restaurants and recreational facilities. The location is ideal for students as it provides easy access to various resources and opportunities in the city.

Q: What is the environment like at your school?

A: The environment at my school is very supportive and encouraging. Students are motivated by both peers and teachers to strive for excellence while being provided with guidance needed to reach their potential.

Q: What did you like about your secondary or high school?

A: The location of my school was what I liked most. As I said, my school was located in the center of my hometown, and therefore it was quite easy for us to go shopping after school. More interestingly, sometimes during the lunch break, we could go to the cinema nearby.

Q: Tell me about anything you didn't like about your school/

A: I do not really want to complain about my high school, and however there were so many things that I disliked about my high school especially some irrational rules there. One of them was that all students must wear school uniforms from Monday to Friday, which drove me crazy. Students are not soldiers and wearing school uniform every day does not make any sense.

Q: Do you think your school is a good place to study?

A: Yes, I believe my school is an excellent place to study. It offers a supportive learning environment, well-qualified teachers, and a wide range of resources that cater to diverse learning styles. Additionally, the emphasis on both academic and personal development prepares students effectively for future challenges.

Q: How do you think your school could be improved?

A: If my high school had offered more optional courses, we would have had a more colorful school life. The current situation is that students there can only take some regular courses, which makes students' lives full of stress, tiredness, and vapidity.
