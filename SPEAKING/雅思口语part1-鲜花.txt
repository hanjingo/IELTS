Q: Do you like to have flowers in your home?

A: Absolutely! I usually put fresh flowers that are in season, like red roses, white lilies, and sunflowers on the coffee table in the living room. I think flowers highlight the decoration of my apartment and impress people.

Q: Where would you go to buy flowers?

A: I usually buy flowers at the florist around the corner down the street near my neighborhood, where I pass by every day. The owner sometimes gives me a discount because I have become one of his patrons. But the most wonderful place to shop for flowers is the flower market, which is held downtown around Spring Festival once a year. An abundance of flowers can be seen there and people gather there to select flowers to decorate their houses and wish for good luck for the New Year.

Q: On what occasions would you give someone flowers?

A: On special days like Mother's Day, and Teacher's Day, I would send carnations, which represent love and respect, to my mom and my teachers. I guess it's the same case in Western culture. And I send flowers to my family and close friends on their birthdays. Just like last week, my best friend Jessica got nine sunflowers from me, which are her favorite. The digit nine symbolizes eternity and I wish our friendship lasts forever.

Q: What kinds of flowers do you know?

A: I'm familiar with various flowers including roses, daisies, carnations, and orchids. Each type has its unique aesthetic and is favored for different occasions due to its distinct appearance and symbolism.

Q: Are flowers important in your culture?

A: Definitely. Flowers do play a significant role in our culture. I can tell that because flowers open out before us everywhere. You know, plants like orchids, chrysanthemums, bamboo, and plum blossoms, which are complimented as 'the 4 noble ones', are popular subjects for Chinese painters for their refined beauty and purity. And the funniest thing is that the 4 noble ones are even depicted in mahjong titles.

Q: Are there any flowers that have special meanings in China?

A: Certainly, in China, flowers like the peony, which symbolizes prosperity and good fortune, and the lotus, representing purity and spiritual enlightenment, hold significant cultural values and are deeply embedded in traditional beliefs.

Q: Have you planted any flowers?

A: Yes, I have enjoyed planting flowers such as sunflowers and lavender in my garden. This hobby is not only relaxing but also rewarding, as it beautifies my surroundings and improves my mood.

Q: Have you sent flowers to others?

A: Indeed, I frequently send flowers like lilies and roses to friends and family on special occasions. This gesture is a heartfelt way to convey love, respect, or sympathy, enhancing relationships and spreading joy.
