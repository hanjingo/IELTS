One idea that I have thought of to make the planet a better place is to advocate for international clean energy regulations, which people has to follow.

By this, I mean implementing a few simple yet strict laws that the society in the world must comply to minimize energy consumption and waste whenever possible.

I really started to think about this deeply about half a decade ago, when I come to the realization that many companies and people in the world are creating a lot of waste and pollution because of their ignorance.

Let me explain this a bit more clearly with an example.

The world now has energy-efficient LED lights and rechargeable batteries, yet the stores are still selling energy-efficient light and disposable batteries, and people still buy them.

This kind of energy-inefficient light not only wastes electricity but also the chemicals and materials in these products are really harmful to the environment.

Even though people are allowed to recycle their batteries nowadays, many of them still end up in landfills and the acid is causing major damage to the water and land with 8 billion people on earth.

I believe it is gross negligence to overlook this issue.

In fact, such regulation will be cost-efficient for people and society in the long run.

So money really shouldn't be an excuse.

I believe by banning the energy inefficient light and disposable batteries, the planet would be a better place in both flora and fauna, as well as people would live healthier lives.

I think the reason why such regulation hasn't been implemented yet is because of our greed and ignorance.

To make this happen, people and the government must unite together again, as they did to ban plastic shopping bags.
