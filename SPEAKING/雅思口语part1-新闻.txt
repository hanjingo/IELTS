Q: Are you interested in news?

A: Yes, I am interested in the news because it keeps me informed about world events and developments in various fields such as politics, science, and technology. Staying updated helps me make informed decisions and contributes to my overall awareness.

Q: How do you usually find news?

A: I typically access news through a combination of online news portals, social media, and news apps on my smartphone. These platforms provide convenient and immediate access to global news, making it easy to stay informed on the go.

Q: How do your friends get news?

A: My friends primarily get their news from social media and news apps. They find these methods convenient as they are integrated into their daily use of smartphones and computers, allowing them to access news effortlessly throughout the day.

Q: Have you read the news this morning?

A: Yes, I read the news this morning. I usually check major headlines and read a few articles of interest from both local and international sources to start my day informed about current events.

Q: Do you often talk with your friends about the news?

A: Yes, discussing news with my friends is a common activity. It helps us share different perspectives and stay engaged with important topics. These discussions can be enlightening and help deepen our understanding of complex issues.
