Q: When do people give gifts or presents in your country?

A: Well, Chinese people send gifts on many festivals and occasions: housewarming, graduation days, wedding anniversaries. In particular, most gifts are exchanged during the Spring Festival, which is a traditional festival celebrating the Chinese Lunar New Year.

Q: Do you ever take a gift when you visit someone in their home?

A: Well, I guess it will be more thoughtful to do so. Most people see it as a kind act if you bring a small gift when visiting someone. It shows that you appreciate being there. Just like an old saying goes, "Courtesy demands reciprocity", which signifies bringing gifts to each other is a good way to sustain a friendship.

Q: When did you last receive a gift?

A: Speaking of the last gift, it is a letter I got from my parents in my first year of university. Considering that I suffered a lot from severe homesickness, they said a lot to comfort me. It gave me a feeling that my parents were with me, giving me the courage and care I needed badly. This gift is of special sentimental value to me.

Q: Do you enjoy looking for gifts for people?

A: I guess not. It is a waste of time making efforts to get the perfect gift. What's more, if the recipient is already wealthy, it will be more difficult to pick up the right present. Still, I have little time to consider what they prefer. I am sort of tight-fisted when it refers to the payment.
