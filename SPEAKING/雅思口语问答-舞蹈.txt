Q: Do you enjoy dancing?

A: Yes, I really do. It doesn't matter what kind of music, but my favorite dance is free dance in a disco club with my friends. I don't know how good I am, but it's just a lot of fun to get out there and shake it all right long.

Q: Has anyone ever taught you to dance?

A: Yes. I was taught in a dancing club. You know, I started learning Latin dance three years ago. I took classes in a club on weekends. The instructor taught us the fundamental steps and skills. He was professional, strict, and patient as well.

Q: Tell me about any traditional dancing in your country?

A: I think one of the traditional dances in China is the lion dance. It's an important tradition in China. Usually, the dance is part of festivities like Chinese New Year, the openings of restaurants, and weddings. If well-performed, the lion dance is believed to bring luck and happiness.

Q: Do you think that traditional dancing will be popular in the future?

A: Yes, I think so. Traditional dancing will definitely be popular because more and more focus has been put on preserving tradition. Nowadays, dancing clubs have seen the increasing number of traditional dance fans and more traditional dance competitions are held.
