Q: What scenery can you see from the window of your home or dormitory?

A: My house is by a large public square. When looking out of the window, I can see lots of people walking around. To be honest, it's very noisy. So I usually close my window in the hall day.

Q: Do you like to watch the scenery from your window?

A: No, because my house is by the public square. When standing by the window, I can hear some noise, even if the window is closed. It makes me upset.

Q: Do you want to live in a house with beautiful window views?

A: Of course, I would like to have a house with a big garden and many trees around. But I think it's just a daydream to me because of the high price of the house in this area.

Q: How do you feel when you can't see any beautiful view from your window?

A: A house without any beautiful view has no soul. It will look like a prison. Hence I avoid renting any underground flat.
