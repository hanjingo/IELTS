Q: What sorts of food do you like eating most?

A: My answer must be Hunan cuisine, which is popular in the southern part of China, just simply because it is the food that people in my hometown eat every day. Therefore, I am quite familiar with the flavor of Hunan cuisine. People cook Hunan food by using a lot of salt, and spicy sauce. As for me, it is the kind of food that makes me feel at home.

Q: Who normally does the cooking in your home?

A: It is my mother who is responsible for cooking three meals for us as a daily routine. Like my father, my mother does not work and she is a full-time housewife. As I said, she prepares three meals a day and fruit for us. Of course, being a housewife is also a tough job.

Q: Do you watch cookery programs on TV?

A: I am not a big fan of cooking. I usually eat outside with my family, cooking at home is what my mother often does. So I never watch cookery programs on TV.

Q: In general, do you prefer eating out or eating at home?

A: I seldom eat at home because of busy work, and instead eating out is what I often do. I wish I could eat at home. However, the fact is that there is no time to cook and wash dishes.
