Q: How often do you go to a restaurant?

A: I usually go to restaurants about once or twice a month, it's a special treat for me and my family.

A: I like going to restaurants during weekends or holidays, like birthdays or spring festivals.

Q: Do you prefer to eat in restaurants or at home?

A: I prefer eating at home because home-cooked meals are healthier and more comforting.

Q: Do you like fast food restaurants? Why or why not?

A: I really like fast food restaurants because fast food can be convenient, and it's the best choice for working meals.

Q: If you could own a restaurant, what kind of food would it serve?

A: If I could own a restaurant, it would serve a mixed edition of Canton cuisine and Hunan dishes. I think it's important to offer different types of food to different people.
