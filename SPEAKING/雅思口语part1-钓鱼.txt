Q: Do you like eating fish?

A: I love eating fish, although I don't like dealing with fish bones. I like simple white fish or salmon, served with a light sauce. Fish is apparently very good for you and has a number of essential oils in it. I actually prefer sea and river fish to other types of seafood like crabs or lobsters.

Q: Is fishing popular in your country?

A: Fishing is indeed quite popular in my country. It's a recreational activity enjoyed by many, especially in coastal regions and near lakes or rivers. Many families have a tradition of going fishing together on weekends.

Q: Have you ever been to a place where there are lots of fish around you?

A: Absolutely, I've had the chance to visit some picturesque lakeside locations where fish abound. It's a serene experience, connecting with nature. The sparkling water and the gentle sound of the waves make it a perfect getaway.

Q: Have you seen any movies featuring lots of fish?

A: Yes, I've seen several movies featuring underwater worlds teeming with fish. These documentaries often reveal the astonishing diversity of marine life, which can be both mesmerizing and educational. So, I suppose a documentary is like a movie really...oh, I have actually seen Finding Nemo, which is an animation, a really funny one, featuring a lot of fish and sea creatures.
