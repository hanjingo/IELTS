Q: Describe a person who likes to read a lot. You should say: Who is this person? How did you know him/her? What he/she likes to read? And explain why you think he/she likes to read a lot?

A: I'd like to talk about my college roommate, Huang, who is a passionate reader. I met Huang when we were assigned to the same dorm room in our first year of university. From day one, I noticed how many books she had brought with her. Huang enjoys reading a wide range of books. She loves fantasy and science fiction novels and loves to read about hair styling, too, often staying up late to finish a captivating story or learn a new hairdressing technique. She also reads a lot of popular science books, especially those about psychology and neuroscience, which relate to her major. What surprised me was her interest in classical literature from around the world. I think Huang reads a lot for several reasons. Firstly, it's her main form of entertainment. She prefers diving into a good book over watching TV or playing video games. Reading seems to truly excite her imagination. Secondly, I believe she uses books as a way to understand different perspectives and cultures. She once told me that reading lets her live many lives and experience things she might never encounter in real life. Lastly, I think reading helps her relieve stress. Whenever she feels overwhelmed with coursework, she turns to her books for comfort and escape. Huang's love for reading has influenced me positively. She's introduced me to many great books I might not have picked up otherwise. Her enthusiasm for discussing what she's read has made me appreciate literature more deeply. Seeing how much joy and knowledge Huang gains from reading has inspired me to read more widely. It's shown me that reading can be both fun and intellectually stimulating, and we can learn practical things, too.
