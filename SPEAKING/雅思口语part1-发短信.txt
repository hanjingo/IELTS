Q: Do you like texting?

A: I'm afraid not, with the development of technology, voice and video messages is more popular than text messages. for example, WeChat is an instant message app, you can use it to chat or have a video with your friends. it is really easy to use it, even if you are illiterate.

Q: Do you prefer sending or receiving messages?

A: I prefer receiving messages. When the person I am chatting with sends more messages than I do, I can feel his or her interest in communicating with me and therefore have more sense of security to share more stories with him or her.

Q: Have you ever received a confusing text message?

A: Quite often. The most confusing text message is a friend who hasn't talked to me for 5 or 10 years all of a sudden sends me a message to borrow some money from me. In most cases, their personal accounts were stolen.

Q: In what circumstances is making a phone call better than sending a text message?

A: I think when you are driving or doing some emergency work, it's better to make a phone call instead of sending a text message.
