Q: Describe a skill you learned in your childhood. What skill did you learn? When did you learn the skill? How did you practise and develop this skill? Why do you think it's essential to learn such skills?

A: The skill I learned during my childhood was cooking. I started learning how to cook when I was about 10 years old. My mother was my first teacher, and I used to assist her in the kitchen during weekends or holidays. Initially, I was responsible for simple tasks like chopping vegetables or stirring pots. Over time, my mother taught me basic recipes such as making rice and simple noodles. As I grew older, I began experimenting with new recipes and techniques inspired by cookbooks and cooking shows. Practicing regularly, observing my mother's methods, and taking small risks in trying new ingredients helped me refine my skills. I believe cooking is an essential skill as it promotes self-sufficiency and healthier eating habits. It is also a creative outlet that brings joy and fosters bonding with family members. Additionally, knowing how to cook can be invaluable in times of necessity, such as living away from home or managing dietary preferences. 

Q: How has the skill you learned in childhood added value to your life?

A: Learning to cook has significantly enriched my life. It has allowed me to be independent and self-reliant, especially when I moved to another city for higher studies. Moreover, cooking has become a stress-relieving activity for me. It also provides an opportunity to connect with friends and family during social gatherings by preparing meals together.

Q: Can you share any memorable experiences or challenges you faced while learning this skill in your childhood?

A: One of my most memorable experiences was the first time I made a meal for my family. I accidentally added too much salt to the noodles, which led to a lot of laughter at the dinner table. While initially embarrassing, it taught me the importance of precision in cooking. That mistake helped me become more careful and attentive in the kitchen.
