Q: Do you usually help people around you?

A: Yes, helping those around me is something I really value. Small acts of kindness can make a big difference. I think that people today are sometimes a bit mean when it comes to helping others, especially helping strangers in need. I like to set a good example and help anyone who needs help.

Q: How do you help people around you?

A: I provide assistance to people by running errands, offering emotional support, or volunteering when possible. I also help strangers or people in the street who ask for help or look like they need a helping hand.

Q: Do your parents teach you how to help others?

A: My parents have instilled the value of helping others in me since childhood, but I also learned about the importance of helping others in school, as well. I know that not everyone has this kind of education at home as well as school, so I appreciate my having had this kind of encouragement to help others from my parents as well as teachers.

Q: What have you done to help the elderly?

A: To support the elderly, I've volunteered as an admin at a local IT company, spending time with and teaching the residents computer skills. That was last summer as part of a community volunteer initiative. I also quite regularly help an old lady who lives nearby, you know, I help her with shopping and some chores from time to time.
