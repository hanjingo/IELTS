Q: How do you usually travel to work or college?

A: Most of the time, I take the bus or the subway to go to work. There are a lot of buses coming and going, and I don't have to wait for a long time.

Q: Have you always traveled to work or college in the same way?

A: No. If I am in a hurry or when it rains, I will take a taxi. In my city, it is really difficult to get on a bus on rainy days. If I don't want to be late, taking a taxi is the best option.

Q: What do you like about traveling to work or college this way?

A: I like taking a taxi because it is better, and I don't have to be crammed on the Sardinelike bus. As we know, the buses tend to be stuffy in summertime, and I don't feel comfortable when there are too many people around me.

Q: What changes would improve the way you travel to work or college?

A: The change that we are waiting for is to have more buses during the rush hour. If there are more buses, and they come every few minutes, people don't have to struggle to get on the bus.
