Q: Do you like teamwork?

A: Yes, I do. I like working with others and sharing ideas. It often leads to better results. But it depends on the team. When everyone works well together and talks openly, it can be really fun and helpful.

Q: Have you worked with a team or someone else?

A: Yes, I have. I've worked in teams at school and at work. Each time was different, but I learned a lot about how to work well with others. It's been a good way to learn new skills.

Q: What do you think are the benefits of working together, in teams?

A: Working together brings different ideas and skills to the table. It can help get things done faster. You also get better at talking to people and making new friends at work. It's often more fun, I think, than working alone.

Q: What do you hate when working together with others?

A: It's really annoying when some people don't do their fair share of work. It's also hard when people don't talk to each other well. Sometimes, it takes longer to make decisions in a team, which can be tough when there's a tight deadline.
