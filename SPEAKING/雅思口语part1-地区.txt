Q: Which part of your country do most people live in?

A: In Australia the vast majority of the population lives along the coast because of the cooler climate. Moreover, the hot and dry conditions of central Australia persuaded early settlers to establish communities in coastal areas. Currently, all state capitals are located along the coastline of Australia.

Q: Tell me about the main industries there?

A: Australia's main industry is the mining and exportation of its natural resources as it is rich in coal, gold, wheat, and wool, amongst others. Furthermore, in the wake of China's economic growth and subsequent increased demand for natural resources, this industry has become even more significant to Australia.

Q: How easy is it to travel around your country?

A: it is very easy to travel around Australia in any mode of transport. Well-maintained highways, many conveniently located airports, interstate train lines and even affordable boating routes mean that traveling in Australia is both readily available and flexible.

Q: Has your country changed much since you were a child?

A: The most significant changes in Australia to me have been the increased economic performance and technological progress. Due to increased demand for Australian goods from overseas, our economy has boomed. Whereas improvements in technology have made mobile phones and the Internet play an increasing role in Australians' lives.
