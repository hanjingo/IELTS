Q: Which day is your favourite in the week?

A: Saturday. I love Saturdays. I feel really free on Saturday and usually do some sports, go out with friends to a restaurant at night and generally let myself go and have fun on Saturdays.

Q: How do you usually spend your time on this day?

A: I usually wake up late, I have a good breakfast, I meet with friends in a mall and take tea somewhere, and then I go to play basketball or tennis, maybe go to the cinema. I'm always pretty active on Saturdays.

Q: Which day is your least favorite in the week?

A: My least favorite is actually Sunday. Especially Sunday evening, as I feel a sense of dread that my weekend is over and my working week is about to begin. I actually even feel a bit depressed on Sundays. It's silly, I know, but hey, that's how I feel!

Q: Is there anything you do every day?

A: I have some fairly strict routines, to be honest. I often get up at the same time each day, around 8 AM. I get dressed, of course, have a shower, and then I do some studies in the morning, perhaps go for a run a few times a week on campus. These are the main routines I have, not necessarily in that order!
