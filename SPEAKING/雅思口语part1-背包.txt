Q: Do you usually carry a bag?

A: Yes, I carry a bag almost always. A simple bag really, in which I put my wallet, my bank cards, stuff like this - my phone... tissues... random daily things.

Q: What kinds of bags do you use when traveling?

A: I use a suitcase mainly - if I'm going on a normal journey, you know, a holiday or something, or back to see my family at Spring Festival. I took a large suitcase that I bought in a store in Guangzhou. And a handbag, and sometimes a backpack, too.

Q: Do you often change bags?

A: No, not really. I'm not someone who buys new bags and accessories very often. I usually buy a couple of good quality bags, a decent enough suitcase, and a backpack and they last years.

Q: Did you use a backpack when you were a child?

A: Yes, definitely. To go to school. For my school books, my PE kit - you know, my sports clothes and things. I used a backpack for most of my school life, actually.

Q: Would you give others bags as gifts?

A: Yes, I think so. Bags are quite common gifts - especially purses and handbags for girls and women. Designer bags are very popular in China today, too. So, wealthy people will often buy bags for their family or friends as luxury gifts.
