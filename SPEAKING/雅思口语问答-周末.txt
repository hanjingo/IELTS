Q: How do you usually spend your weekends?

A: I normally spend my weekends doing something relaxing, because I tend to be busy from Monday to Friday. I tend to keep a little notebook and jot down things to do as they occur to me so that I can plan Saturday's activity and I don't waste time. On Sundays, I do nothing because that is my relaxing day.

Q: Which is your favorite part of the weekend?

A: Sunday is my best day of the weekend because apart from cooking food or perhaps doing some washing of clothes, I do nothing. I saunter down to the village shop to get my Sunday newspaper and chat with folks on the way. I like to spend time reading the Sunday paper because it is much thicker that day and has many different supplements.

Q: Do you think your weekends are long enough?

A: No, of course not. Sometimes I wish the weekend included Friday or Monday because I use up Saturday doing necessary things so only Sunday is free. With an extra day, I could go on a 2-day holiday break.

Q: How important do you think it is to have free time at the weekends?

A: Free time is very important because if work takes up 7 days there will be something wrong with your work or life balance. If you do not relax on Saturday and Sunday, you will return to work on Monday just as tired as you left work on Friday.
