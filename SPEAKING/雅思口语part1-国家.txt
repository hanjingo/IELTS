Q: Which part of your country do most people live in?

A: China is a highly urbanized country. In other words, most Chinese people live in urban city areas as opposed to living in rural areas. Anyway, most Chinese live in the Shanghai city, followed by Guangzhou and Beijing.

Q: Tell me about the main industries there?

A: China is one of the most industrialized nations in the world, and one of the richest in that because of its spectacular industrialization efforts over the past ten years. China is famous for its manufacturing industries, and the main products are cheap for the consumers.

Q: How easy is it to travel around your country?

A: Having one of the best road transportation systems, if not the best, in the world along with one of the fastest and most reliable rail services, China makes it incredibly easy to travel around the country at an affordable cost. Besides, China also has a very efficient air traveling system in place for those who don't want to travel by road and railway system.

Q: Has your country changed much since you were a child?

A: China is not the same country anymore as it was 40 years ago. One of the most noticeable changes is, in my opinion, China has a higher GDP level now than at any time in its history. Besides, China has also become more industrialized and developed over the years playing a crucial role in shaping the future of this world.
