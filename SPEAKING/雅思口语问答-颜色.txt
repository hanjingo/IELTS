Q: What's your favorite color?

A: My favorite color is yellow firstly because it is warm and seems to be able to lift my mood as opposed to cooler colors and secondly it reminds me of the morning sun that also evokes feelings of warmth and love within me.

Q: Do you like the same colors now as you did when you were younger?

A: When I was younger I liked the color red very much, however, this was because I wanted to become a firefighter. Now, as I have matured I value deeper things than my career and hence my favorite color has changed.

Q: What can you learn about a person from the colors they like?

A: I believe you cannot truly learn anything from a person's color preference because each person is individual and to label someone with certain characteristics based upon a color would be short-sighted and an unhelpful generalization.

Q: Do any colors have a special meaning in your culture?

A: In Australia green and gold are symbolic of Australian culture. Although the colors are not in our flag they are taken from a native plant, the Golden Wattle. Green and gold are often seen at many world events, such as the Olympics, where people come to show support for Australia.
