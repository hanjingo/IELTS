I've been played Cross Fire for a few years now.

One of my friends got me into it one the university and I have been playing it ever since.

This is a first-person shooter game and there are several versions of it.

The most recent one is Cross Fire-One Shoot.

Basically, in this game, I'm a soldier who runs around on different maps, shooting enemies.

I really enjoyed the multiplayer area online where I can prove myself against other competencies.

I like to get really competitive and I think I'm pretty good at it.

I have a great ranking and it's always a lot of excitement when I get a couple of hours to play.

I usually play in the evenings and on the weekends a few times each week.

I would say around five hours or so.

I'm getting quite busy with studies these days, so I don't get as much time to play as I used to.

My favorite part is not only really exciting, but it also requires a lot of strategy to outsmart other players.

The graphics and sounds are outstanding.

The game develops quick analytic thinking as well as hand-eye coordination while having tons of fun.

I don't think this game is for everyone because it's quite violent and fast, but however, if a person is really into FPS games, Cross Fire is definitely a game to try.

I'm sure we'll be playing these games with my friends for a few more years to play.
