Q: Can you fix things?

A: I'm pretty good at fixing and repairing things, but really, it depends on what type of things. Nowadays, so much is digital. I am not sure how to fix things that might go wrong - like my phone, or computer or so. But I used to be good at fixing my bicycle!

Q: What kinds of things can you fix?

A: Mechanical things mainly - things like my bike... em... I'm also pretty good at fixing things around the home that might go wrong, like pictures or a wobbly chair leg. Basic things like that.

Q: Have you ever asked people to teach you how to fix things?

A: Not that I can remember... let me think... I think once I asked my mother to help me repair the jeans which I had. You know, she had a sewing kit - needle and thread etc. So, yeah, she taught me how to sew, come to think of it. I can sew quite well. You know, holes in my socks and things like that!

Q: Do you think fixing things is a necessary skill?

A: Nowadays, I don't think it's that essential, really. People don't really fix things often. We live in quite a throw-away society these days, which is quite wasteful, really. People don't repair things when they go wrong. Also, many things need experts to repair them and are not easy for everyday folk like me to fix.
