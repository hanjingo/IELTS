Q: Do you like eating ice cream?

A: I do. I love eating ice cream. Recently, in my city, there are quite a few places that specialize in ice cream, offering all sorts of different and interesting flavors, from pistachio to mango, to green tea, to raspberry chocolate flavor - loads of different types!

Q: Did you eat ice cream when you were a child?

A: I didn't really eat so much ice cream as a child because there wasn't really any ice cream available in my town. There were types of ice lollies on sticks that you could buy in freezers in shops, but there wasn't really any real ice cream.

Q: Are there any shops selling ice cream near the place where you live?

A: I now live in quite a big city, and there are many shops in the local mall that sell ice cream, which has very different flavors on sale.

Q: Can you make ice cream by yourself?

A: I have no idea how to make ice cream! I believe you need to have a special machine, and I'm not totally sure what ingredients you need to make it. Possibly cream or milk, and chopped fruits... I'm not sure to be honest.
