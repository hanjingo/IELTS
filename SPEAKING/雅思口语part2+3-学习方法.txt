Q: Talk about an idea you have had to make your learning more effective.

A: The technique I have to enhance my learning experience was a technique called repetition, specifically for the vast syllabus of the Civil Services Examination. I came across this concept while reading a book on effective study habits. Space repetition is a technique that involves reviewing information at increasing intervals, allowing for better retention and long-term memory. I first thought about this concept in my final year of university when I was preparing for my Civil Services Examination, which is a highly competitive and comprehensive exam that assesses the candidates' knowledge about various topics like history, geography, and current affairs. I realized that cramming information the night before the exam was enough to grasp and retain the whole syllabus. By implementing space repetition, I create a study schedule that incorporates regular reviews of key concepts and facts of the study subject. Instead of focusing solely on recent topics, I review previously covered material and gradually increase the gap between each review session. What encouraged me to think about this idea was the vast syllabus of the Indian Civil Services Examination and the need to develop a more effective learning strategy. I was inspired by the research and success stories of previous candidates who have utilized space repetition for their preparation and have achieved good results in their exams. To further improve this idea, I plan to leverage technology by using specialized space repetition software or an app tailored to the India Civil Services Examination. These tools will help me prioritize and monitor the topics, set up customized review intervals, and track my progress. By integrating technology into my study routine, I believe I can optimize the implementation of spaced repetition and make my learning even more efficient and targeted. In conclusion, incorporating the spaced repetition technique into my study routine has proven to be a valuable strategy. I'm motivated to continue refining this technique with better technology and leveraging the benefits of spaced repetition to master the syllabus and excel in the examination.
