Q: Do you think happiness is important?

A: Absolutely, happiness is crucial! It's the spice of life, isn't it? Happiness doesn't just make life more enjoyable; it also boosts our health, enhances our relationships, and increases our productivity. Cultivating happiness is essential for a well-rounded and fulfilling life.

Q: Do you think people are happy when buying new things?

A: "Yes, buying new things can definitely bring a burst of happiness. It's that initial thrill of acquiring something you've wanted, the excitement of a new possession. However, this happiness is often fleeting and short-lived...True, lasting happiness usually comes from deeper, more meaningful experiences and connections."
