The State Technological University of Rajiv Gandhi Vishwavidyalaya, the RGPV is a public building that I visit regularly for my undergraduate studies.

The university is located in the Northwestern part of Bhopal and it's not too far from the IT park.

It is of course a post-secondary institution that offers several different degree programs, including computer science, which is my major.

It is relatively a new building that was established and constructed at the end of the 20th century, so it is a couple of decades old.

The main building has a large glass entranceway and a stone roof and there are four stories in height.

It has a lot of natural lights flooding into the main area and the classrooms, and it is air-conditioned, so it stays nice and cool on a hot summer day.

Each day, hundreds of students teachers, and researchers visit the building to learn teach and research new technologies.

Of course, there are lots of modern equipment throughout the building.

It is wired with fiber optics for lightning-fast internet and there are several computer rooms both with Apple and PC for students and teachers to use.

I think the building is quite unique in construction with a large window and the dome roof.

Also, there are really nice stone floors and tiers inside the main building for students to socialize.

Personally, I really like the building as it creates a positive learning experience for me where I can focus on my class work as well as my computer work.

I really like visiting the building to socialize and network with my peers.
