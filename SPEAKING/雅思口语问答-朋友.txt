Q: How often do you go out with friends?

A: Almost every day, I hang out with my friends since I am a college student who is living in the dorm on my campus. All my roommates are my friends, and we take courses together, have meals together, and even do all the other things together.

Q: Tell me about your best friend at school?

A: My best friend in my university was the boy who was living in the dorm on my campus. we play computer games together for the whole night.

Q: How friendly are you with your neighbors?

A: Fairly friendly. There is a typical Chinese family of three people living next door to my apartment. Luckily, the child of this family is my age, and we are classmates and of course good friends. We often visit each other's homes to do homework or watch some movies together.

Q: Which is more important to you, friends or family?

A: Without any hesitation, I choose my family who are obviously much more crucial to me. Family members, especially our parents, do whatever they can do to offer us a better life. In every second of my life, I can feel the love my family members give me. However, sometimes friends are the people who can have a happy time with you, but not who can face troubles with you. Family members are the only people who are always on your side.
