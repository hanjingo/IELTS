Some people think it is more beneficial to play sports that are played in teams, e.g. football. And some people think it is more beneficial to play individual sports, e.g. tennis and swimming. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

It is claimed that team games such as football, and basketball are beneficial to players, meanwhile, others believe that sports played individually have superior advantages. From my perspective, both types of sports are beneficial.

On the one hand, it seems undeniable that compared to an individual sport, a group sport requires collaboration between participants, players should work as a team to share the responsibilities, take into account opportunities, and choose others, so the qualities as cooperation and coordination are of the essence to achieve maximum synergy. For instance, sometimes a player has a leadership position during half of the game and then drops out to ensure the victory to another teammate. Another example, a football player can sacrifice the opportunity to score a goal to pass the ball to his team player, if the chance of a goal being is higher for his teammate.

On the other hand, solo sports also bring a number of benefits that allow to cultivate and maximize their personal capacity. To be precise, being alone without any assistance from others challenges his own limits. For instance, a tennis player should strategize the ball serves, and predict where the ball falls, to be agile in his moment, he should multi-task all the time. Additionally, when playing individually, a person is liberated from constant regulation acts with others, a player should not agree with his action with other participants, thereby, having freedom a player can experiment with other forms of playing. Therefore, these have more choices of satisfaction.

In conclusion, each type of sport has its own merits to shape personal development. It depends on each player as to what type of sports they should do.
