Some people think that governments should subsidize healthy food, while others think that they should tax unhealthy food. Give your view in no more than 250 words.

The issue of taxing food has grown in importance globally. Although taxation on food is a first line of thought, it must not be forgotten that healthy food should be provided cheaper. This essay will discuss both issues using an example from the National Health Service and the UK to demonstrate points and support arguments. 

On the one hand, there is ample evidence to suggest that it is the responsibility of the government to provide healthy food cheaper to the population. The main reason behind this is that by consuming healthy food, several illnesses may decrease tremendously which are associated with unhygienic food. For example, a recent study conducted by the NHS in Manchester showed that the rate of patients admitted to the hospital decreased significantly compared to previous years. Therefore, it is clear that making healthy food cheaper has a positive impact on human health.

On the other hand, a substantial group of people think that unhealthy food should be taxed. This is because taxing heavily on food that is hazardous to human health, will often discourage people from taking it. For instance, across the UK, after the tax on unhealthy food such as alcohol and tobacco had risen, the number of people consuming it plunged dramatically. Thus, it is evident that increasing taxes on unhealthy food discourages people from eating it.

In conclusion, although there are different views about whether or not a tax on unhealthy food, personally, I believe that governments should tax those foods that are harmful to humans so that society will have a better quality of life.
