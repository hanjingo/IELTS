People living in 21st century have a better life quality than the people who lived in the previous centuries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

In recent years, there has been an ongoing debate on whether people living in the 21st century have a better quality of life than those who lived in the previous centuries. In my opinion, I strongly believe that people in the 21st century have a better quality of life, and this can be attributed to various factors.

Firstly, the level of access to education, healthcare, and technology has improved significantly in the 21st century compared to previous centuries. The world is now more interconnected than ever before, and people have access to vast resources and knowledge.  Medical advancements and better healthcare systems have increased life expectancies, and it is now possible to cure diseases that were once fatal.

Secondly, people in the 21st century have access to more job opportunities and a better standard of living. Technological advancements have made it possible for people to work from any location while earning a decent paycheck. The labor market has expanded, and this has created more job opportunities in various fields.

Thirdly, people in the 21st century have advanced social mobility and personal freedom. Prevailing legal frameworks forbid any sort of discrimination. People are free to choose their profession, partner and lifestyle without fear of judgment from society.

Finally, human rights, civil liberties, and democratic values have been established in the 21st century. This has helped in improving the quality of lives for all individuals irrespective of their gender, race religion or color. People now have the right to express their opinions, practice their faith and participate in the decision-making process.

In conclusion, people living in the 21st century have a better quality of life than those who lived in previous centuries. The world has transformed exceedingly over the years, and it has impacted the lives of people positively. With the advancements in technology, education, healthcare, personal freedom, and democratic values, people now have more opportunities and a better standard of living.
