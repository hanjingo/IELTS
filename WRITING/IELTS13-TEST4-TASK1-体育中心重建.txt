The two maps illustrate the current layout of a university sports centre and the proposed changes after redevelopment.

Overall, the redevelopment plan includes significant additions and changes to the existing facilities. The central area containing the Reception, 25m Pool, Gym, and Changing Rooms will remain largely unchanged, while the two outdoor courts will be replaced by new indoor facilities.

Presently, the sports centre features a 25m Pool in the middle, flanked by a Gym to the right and Changing Rooms to the left. There is also a seating area adjacent to the pool.

In the proposed redevelopment, the left side of the sports centre will see the addition of a Sports Shop next to the Reception and a new Changing Room. Beyond the Changing Room, there will be a Leisure Pool.

On the right side of the centre, a Cafe will be introduced next to the Reception. Moving further, a Sports Hall will replace one of the outdoor courts, with a second Changing Room adjacent to it. Additionally, two Dance Studios will be constructed opposite the Sports Hall.

In conclusion, the planned redevelopment will significantly expand the university sports centre by adding various new facilities, enhancing the overall utility and diversity of the centre's offerings.
