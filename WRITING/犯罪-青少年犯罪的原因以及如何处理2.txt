In some countries, a high proportion of criminal acts are committed by teenagers. Why has this happened? What can be done to deal with this? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

The rise in criminal activities among teenagers is a matter of great concern. The reasons for such behavior can be attributed to various social, economic, and cultural factors. Addressing this issue requires a comprehensive approach that involves both prevention and intervention strategies.

One of the primary reasons for the high proportion of criminal acts committed by teenagers is a lack of parental guidance and supervision. In some cases, parents may be absent, preoccupied with work, or simply neglectful. As a result, teenagers may feel neglected, isolated, or unsupported, leading them to engage in criminal behaviors to gain a sense of belonging or attention.

Moreover, modern technology and social media have provided teenagers with access to a wide range of information and communication channels. Sometimes, teenagers get swayed by negative influences and messages on social media, which can encourage them to indulge in criminal activities.

To address this issue, it is essential to strengthen the role of families by promoting parental involvement in their children's lives. This can include providing parenting education programs, mentoring, and coaching to parents. Furthermore, governments can initiate community-based programs aimed at improving life skills, building leadership, and providing positive role models for teenagers.

Moreover, schools can help address this issue by providing structured programs on character education, conflict resolution, and social skills training. Such programs can help students develop positive communication skills, problem-solving abilities, and social-emotional learning.

In conclusion, addressing the issue of the high proportion of criminal acts committed by teenagers requires a joint effort from parents, communities, and governments. A proactive approach, including prevention and intervention strategies, can empower teenagers to make positive choices and live up to their potential.
