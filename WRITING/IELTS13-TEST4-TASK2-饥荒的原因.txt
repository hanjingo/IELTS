One major reason for ongoing hunger is poverty. Many people in developing countries cannot afford to buy sufficient food, even if it is available. Poverty is often exacerbated by factors such as war, political instability, and economic inequality. Additionally, economic inequality means that wealth and resources are concentrated in the hands of a few, leaving large segments of the population without access to basic necessities, including food.
