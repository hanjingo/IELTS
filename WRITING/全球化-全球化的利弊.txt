Some people think the increasing business and cultural contact between countries brings many positive effects. Others say it cause the loss of national identities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

In recent years, globalization has rapidly increased the business and cultural contact among countries. Some people believe that this growth is positive since it enhances the economic, cultural, and social opportunities of countries. Conversely, others argue that such development leads to the loss of national identities. While it is true that globalization may cause drawbacks, it can bring about multiple benefits to countries. 

On the one hand, contact between countries can negatively affect national identities. People should be able to preserve their traditions, customs, and values. Globalization, however, can lead to cultural homogenization, which is the process that makes all cultures look the same. For instance, fast food chains are everywhere in the world, destroying local cuisine. Besides, the increasing use of the internet and social media worldwide could make young people admire other cultures to the point of rejecting their own heritage. It is also true that globalization's effects are not uniform, leading to unequal power between countries.

On the other hand, globalization can bring numerous advantages. Firstly, it could create new business opportunities and markets. As countries share their products, skills, and knowledge, they create a vital economic interdependence. Additionally, multiculturalism can encourage the exchange of cultural practices and facilitate communication between nations. This can lead to new collaborations, i.e., academic and scientific initiatives, and improve immigration policies to encourage diversity. Finally, globalization promotes peaceful relationships among nations as trade and cultural contact can build trust, which would make it hard for countries to engage in conflicts.

As a conclusion, while globalization can indeed bring about cultural homogenization, I do not believe it is a major threat to national identities. Rather, people should view it as a chance to interact with and learn from others while preserving their unique heritage. Ultimately, globalization should be regulated to ensure that it benefits all nations evenly, rather than promoting unequal global relationships.
