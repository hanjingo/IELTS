Economic wealth in rich countries brings happiness. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

At recent years, the economic conditions have been developed in all countries in the world. However, it seems that developing countries have better achievement in increasing their happiness indicator rather than developed countries. I am agree with this statement and I will try to elaborate reasons for this phenomenon in this essay.

Competition is the first reason why people in developed countries are no happy that they were. Due to the economic growth, companies put themself in competition. This condition creates new rules to workers. Employees are forced to work harder than before to avoid being beaten by other companies in terms of technology, quality and quantity. Take mobile phone companies as an example. They compete to develop new technology to attract consumers. People in developing countries, on the other hand, are focused more on building their businesses instead of competing each other because many of the companies are categorized as small to medium companies.

Moreover, people in developed countries already have wealth to enjoy a better standard of living so increased wealth will not add any happiness in their day-to-day life on the other hand, people in developing countries can enjoy a better standard of living due to economic growth and this makes happier, because it is like that when you are already a billionaire and additional of few billion in your wealth doesn't make you happy or sad but when you are earning only few hundred bucks and suddenly your earnings increased upto millions than definitely, people will feel happy with these development.
