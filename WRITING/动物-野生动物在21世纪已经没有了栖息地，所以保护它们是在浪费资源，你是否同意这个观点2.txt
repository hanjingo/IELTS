Wild animals have no place in the 21st century, so protecting them is a wast of resources. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

As the world advances, human society has encroached upon the habitats of wild animals; leading to their decline in numbers. However, some people believe that protecting them is a waste of resources. In my opinion, I disagree with this argument, and I believe that we have a moral duty to keep wild animals safe.

Firstly, wild animals are valuable to the ecosystem. Each species plays a unique role in the cycle of life. They help pollinate plants, provide food for other animals, and help control pest populations. Removing them from the ecosystem would lead to an imbalance and potentially cause widespread disruptions that could be catastrophic for environmental health. Protecting wild animals ensures that this delicate balance is preserved.

Secondly, wild animals have an inherent right to exist. They are sentient beings, capable of experiencing pain, suffering, and joy. It is the responsibility of humans to protect other creatures that share our planet. The preservation of wild animals, therefore, is not just about avoiding ecological harm, but about respecting their rights and acknowledging their intrinsic value.

Finally, protecting wild animals also has significant economic benefits. Tourism and wildlife reserves generate a significant amount of revenue for countries, especially in Africa, where conservation efforts are essential. Furthermore, animals like elephants and rhinos have historical and cultural significance, and protecting them preserves valuable heritage.

In conclusion, protecting wild animals is not a waste of resources, but an ethical obligation, an ecological necessity, and a valuable economic asset. It is up to humans to find ways to coexist with the creatures that share our world and ensure that we leave a healthy and functional planet for generations to come.
