Public service.

Free education and healthcare may be provided by the state.

Governments pay the salaries of public sector workers like police officers and teachers, and the necessary money is raised by taxing people's income.

Other people think that essential services should be free, and governments should pay for them.

Some governments control public transport systems and even TV channels. In other countries, these services are provided by private companies.

Some people believe that competition between private companies is good. It helps to improve quality while bringing prices down.

Punishment.

Corporal punishment creates an atmosphere of fear and anger. Children who are punished physically may become shy or resentful.

Corporal Punishment is not a good idea. Physical punishment is a way of controlling children using fear. This does not promote trust between adults and children.

Printed Publication.

Printed publications have cultural and historical value.

Reading from paper has been proven to be more beneficial for cognitive development than reading from electronic devices.

Radio.

By providing audio information only, radio leaves much room for imagination.

It is undeniable that radio is facing increasing competition from other forms of media. It needs to improve in terms of program variety and reception quality. But its longevity is beyond doubt.

Radio is not likely to be replaced soon or at all because it is the most convenient way to acquire information.
