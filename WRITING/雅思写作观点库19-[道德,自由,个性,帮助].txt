Freedom.

Help.

I accept that it is important to help our neighbors and fellow citizens.

I believe that we have an obligation to help those who live beyond our national borders.

A small donation to an international charity might have a much greater impact than helping in our local area.

Personality.

Growing up in the countryside helps to cultivate one's self-reliance.

Many people argue that we control our own destinies.

Many successful people are "self-made".

Others think that our education and upbringing are more important.

Our personalities and achievements depend more on nurture than nature.

People raised in a city are often competitive and ambitious.

People's character can be attributed to many factors, the environment being only one of them.

Some people believe that our personalities are determined mainly by genetics.

Though possessing individuality, people often share some collective traits unique to the place where they live.

We are not limited by our genetic characteristics or upbringing.

We can shape our own personalities.

We inherit our abilities and talents from our parents.
