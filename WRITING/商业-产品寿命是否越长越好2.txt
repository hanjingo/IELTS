Some people think products should be made to last as long as possible, while others think making products cheap is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

In today's society, there is a growing debate regarding the durability of products. While some people advocate for making high-quality products that last longer, others argue that producing cheap products is more valuable. In this essay, I will discuss both viewpoints and provide my perspective on the issue.

On the one hand, there are advocates for durable products. They believe that making products that are long-lasting is more ethical and environmentally sustainable. For example, producing long-lasting goods can significantly reduce waste and slow down the depletion of natural resources. Furthermore, making high-quality products instills positive brand recognition and loyalty, which subsequently leads to a more significant market share and increased profits.

On the other hand, there are those who argue that producing cheap goods is more beneficial. They believe that people want affordability and are willing to overlook quality for reduced prices. This can be seen in the fast-fashion industry, where companies prioritize lowering production costs and selling cheaper clothes. Moreover, producing low-cost products can cater to a broader market, appealing to consumers with lower incomes, and can also benefit the economy by increasing the demand for unskilled labor.

In my opinion, while there are benefits to both sides, prioritizing durability over cheapness is preferable. This is because producing high-quality products reduces waste and environmental impact, leading to a safer and more sustainable planet. Moreover, goods that last longer also deliver better value for consumers and could lead to increased brand loyalty, providing a steady stream of revenue to companies.

In conclusion, while there are valid arguments on both sides, building long-lasting goods should be prioritized. The benefits of sustainability, better value, and customer loyalty are just a few of the reasons why companies should focus on making high-quality, long-lasting products.
