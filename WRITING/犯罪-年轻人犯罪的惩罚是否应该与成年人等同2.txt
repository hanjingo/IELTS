Young people who commit crimes should be treated in the same way as adults by authorities. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

The issue of whether young offenders should be treated the same way as adults by authorities has been a highly debated topic. While some argue that young people should be held accountable for their crimes like adults, others believe that their age should be taken into account. In my opinion, I strongly disagree with the former.

Firstly, young people are still in the process of maturing and developing their moral judgment. This means that the responsibility for their actions should also be considered in this context. Young people may not fully understand the consequences of their actions, unlike adults who are aware of the ramifications of their misdeeds. Therefore, young people should be treated differently to adults so that their rehabilitation and reintegration into society can be monitored and approached in a way that takes into account their immaturity.

Secondly, the severity of the punishment should be measured according to the nature and impact of the offense. Young people should be given a different sentence because the focus of the criminal justice system should be to reform and not to punish them severely. The focus on rehabilitation ensures that the young offender remains accountable for their actions and learns from their mistake without impacting their future negatively.

In conclusion, it is inappropriate to treat young offenders the same way as adults as factors such as immaturity and the context of the offense should be taken into consideration. Instead, the criminal justice system should adopt a more rehabilitative approach for young offenders to help them learn from their mistakes and become responsible adults in the future.
