In recent years, there has been a growing concern in many countries regarding the high proportion of cirminal acts committed by teenagers. Various factors contribute to this issue, and it has become a significant social problem. In this essay, we will discuss the reasons for this phenomenon and suggest measures to address it.

One of the significant reasons for the rising number of criminal activities by teenagers is the increasing influence of technology. Today, the younger generation is highly exposed to technology and social media, which often leads them to engage in cyberbullying, online harassment, and other digital offenses. Moreover, social media platforms have a pervasive impact on their lives, and they often get involved in violent and aggressive behavior in real life, influenced by what they witness on social media.

Another reason for the increasing prevalence of juvenile delinquency is the lack of parental supervision. Parents are either too busy at work or have a relaxed parenting style, which results in children having more freedom than they can handle. This often leads to unsupervised activities and unsupervised time spent on social media platforms that can lead to unhealthy activities.

Furthermore, the socioeconomic situation of a country also has a significant impact on juvenile delinquency. Some teenagers grow up in poverty-stricken areas and deal wiht family and school pressures that can lead them towards crime.

To address the problem of juvenile delinquency, several steps need to be taken. First, parents must step up and be more engaged in their children's lives, keep tabs on their online behavior and offer adequate supports and guidance whenever necessary. Second, governments must focus on providing better education to underprivileged children and  provide them with the means to escape the harsh realities of poverty. Finally, law enforcement agencies need to be adequately resourced and equipped to address juvenile delinquency and should prioritize prevention over prosecution.

In conclusion, juvenile delinquency is an issue that requires urgent attention, and it's essential to identify the causes and find meaningful solutions. Addressing the root causes of this social problem requires collaborative efforts between parents, schools, law enforcement agencies, and the government to provide a better environment for teenagers. By tackling the problem of juvenile delinquency, we can create a safer and better future for all.
