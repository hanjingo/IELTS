Some people think it is more beneficial to play sports that are played in teams, e.g. football. And some people think it is more beneficial to play individual sports, e.g. tennis and swimming. Discuss about both views and give your own opinion.

It is argued that the advantage of playing sport are more in a group or in their individual. In this essay, I will discuss this two sides of view and conclude with my personal opinion.

First of all, playing a sport within a team can achieve the best result and support each team members. This means a group of people try to do their best in the competition because of the same goal, it gathers people to be together and build a strong relationship. In addition, a sports team has more chance to share their trouble of playing sport with other players and the problem might be solved easier than individual sports. For example, football is a sport that needs lots of people to deliver a ball with the team's cooperation and make the game successful. As well as analyzing the weaknesses and strengths of each player and put them in different positions which is a way to overcome some people's weaknesses.

On the other hand, individual sports can be more freedom which means the person can make whatever effort they want and there are no team members to be concerned about. However, it can be stressful for someone who plays individual sports. It gathered the spotlight on only one person and the player needs to face the situation alone if they lose the game. For instance, people who swim can achieve the final result by individual training but might feel more frustrated than team sport players as they practice by themself without group support.

In conclusion, every sport has its positive and negative sides. However, I believe the benefits of group sports outweigh individual play as I think a team can build a strong power.
