People living in 21st century have a better life quality than the people who lived in the previous centuries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

Life has changed significantly over the years, and each generation has contributed to the progress of society. The 21st century is a testimony to how far humans have come in terms of advancements in technology, medicine, and infrastructure among other things. However, the question remains whether people living in the present have a higher quality of life than those who lived in previous centuries.

From a medical standpoint, people in the 21st century have certainly benefited. Advancements in medicine and healthcare have led to a longer life expectancy, reduced malnutrition, and healthier lifestyles. Infectious diseases that used to be fatal, like smallpox or polio, are now preventable, and medication has improved vastly to mitigate potentially life-threatening illnesses. Further, there have been significant developments in assisted reproductive technologies, which have opened new doors for individuals and couples seeking parenthood.

The improvement in technology also confers benefits. The internet and other digital innovations have made the world a more connected and convenient place. People can now socialize with anyone, anywhere in the world through social media, video chats, or instant messaging. Additionally, automation in industries has led to better efficiency, thus creating more job opportunities and higher living standards.

On the other hand, many argue that the people living in previous centuries had a better quality of life. In those eras, families had strong bonds and living situations often resulted in a greater sense of community. Moreover, there was more emphasis on interpersonal relationships and less dependency on material possessions. People had more time for family and leisure activities than present-day lifestyles allow.

In conclusion, while people living in earlier centuries may have their reasons for considering their way of life better, the people of the 21st century have enjoyed significant advancements in technology, healthcare, and social progress. Therefore, it is fair to state that people living in the present enjoy a better quality of life than those who lived in previous centuries.
