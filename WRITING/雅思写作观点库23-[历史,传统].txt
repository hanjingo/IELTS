History.

History is undeniably important for providing context and understanding our past.

Learning about world history encourages tolerance and appreciation for different cultures and civilizations. 

Studying History allows individuals to learn from past mistakes, understand societal changes, and appreciate the progress humanity has made over time.

Studying world history allows students to see the bigger picture of human development and progress.

The study of local history provides unique insights into the development of one's community and identity.

Traditional.

Architecture from the past is often linked with certain historic events, the description of which may sometimes be found in history books. 

Because of industrialization and global trade, many traditions have disappeared.

Clothes are mass-produced in standard sizes.

Historic relics should be preserved for their historical significance.

It is important to maintain our different cultural identities.

Religious and traditional festivals have certainly become times for celebration.

Some traditional views and values are certainly applicable to the modern world.

Many of the ideas that elderly people have about life are becoming less relevant for younger people.

Many older people indeed believe in traditional values that often seem incompatible with the needs of younger people.

The old and the new need not be in mortal conflict, They can co-exist in harmony.

Traditional customs are still important during weddings and religious festivals.

We should celebrate festivals, teach traditional skills, and protect historic places.
