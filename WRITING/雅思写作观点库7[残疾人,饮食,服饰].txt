Disabled People.

Discrimination against disabled people is illegal in many countries.

People with disabilities should be treated the same as everybody else. They should have the same rights as other people. They should have access to the same jobs as other citizens.

Ramps and lifts for wheelchairs should be installed in public buildings.

Diet.

Importing food can have economic consequences, as local farmers may suffer if they cannot compete against cheaper imported produce.

Imported food often requires long distances to travel to reach its destination, resulting in high carbon emissions.

One advantage of eating food from all over the world is the wider variety that is available.

People need information about what foods contain. Food packaging must show the food's nutritional content.

There should be more time for sports on school timetables. Unhealthy junk food should be banned from school menus.

Transportation and packaging of food from the other side of the world incur environmental costs, from increased carbon emissions to the usage of single-use plastics.

Dress.

Students are in no danger of losing their individuality by wearing uniforms as the kind of clothes we wear is only a slight indication of our personality.

Students won't engage in some vain and vicious competition about who has the best clothing.

The Uniform gives the students a sense of community.
