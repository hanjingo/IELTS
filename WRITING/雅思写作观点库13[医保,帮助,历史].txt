Healthcare.

Many people prefer to pay more for personal health service.

State healthcare is paid by the government using money from taxes. Good healthcare should be available to everyone for free. The National Health Service in the UK provides free healthcare for every resident.

State hospitals are often very large and difficult to run.

Patients have their own rooms and more comfortable facilities.

Private healthcare is unfair because only wealthy people can afford it. Everyone has access to the same quality of care and treatment.

Private hospitals have shorter waiting lists for operations and appointments; patients can benefit from faster treatment.

Help.

I accept that it is important to help our neighbors and fellow citizens.

I believe that we have an obligation to help those who live beyond our national borders.

A small donation to an international charity might have a much greater impact than helping in our local area.

History.

Learning about world history encourages tolerance and appreciation for different cultures and civilizations. 

Studying world history allows students to see the bigger picture of human development and progress.

The study of local history provides unique insights into the development of one's community and identity.
