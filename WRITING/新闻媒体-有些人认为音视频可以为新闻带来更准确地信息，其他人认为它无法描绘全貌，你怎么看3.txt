Some people think visual images (like photographs and videos) can tell information more accurately in a news story, while others think they cannot represent the full story. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

In the era of digital media, visual images such as photographs and videos are playing an increasingly significant role in conveying news stories. While some people believe that visual images are more accurate in representing a news story, others argue that these images fail to represent the full context behind the story. This essay will explore both views and express my own opinion on this matter.

On the one hand, many people believe that visual images are an essential tool in presenting news stories accurately. Visual images, such as videos, often capture the atmosphere and emotions surrounding a story that written articles cannot do. This can improve the viewer's understanding of the story and leave a more lasting impression. Additionally, visual images can be verified easily if they depict events like protests or riots, where eyewitnesses or journalists use cameras to document everything that is happening.

On the other hand, some people opine that visual images do not represent the full story in news, and they can be easily manipulated. Videos and photographs can be edited or cropped to portray a particular bias, which can significantly misinterpret the news story, making the viewers think about a particular issue in a particular way. Furthermore, photographs and videos can only work to convey certain types of stories, leaving many news stories, such as investigative news or academic research, unrepresented.

In my opinion, visual images can be useful in telling a news story, but they should not be relied upon entirely. News organizations' ethical responsibility must be to present a balanced and unbiased view of events, and they must ensure that the visual images they use in their reports are accurate and provide the full context of the situation. Reporters have the obligation to combine both visual and written information, ensuring viewers have a complete and nuanced understanding of news stories.

In conclusion, visual images like photographs and videos are useful tools in telling a news story, but they cannot substitute written articles. News organizations must be cautious about relying solely on visual images to report news stories and ensure that they present a balanced view to avoid any misinterpretation or bias.
