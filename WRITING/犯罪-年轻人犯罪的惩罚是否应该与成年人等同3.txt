Young people who commit crimes should be treated in the same way as adults by authorities. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

Crime is a social problem that is prevalent in every part of the world. Young people are often held responsible for their wrongdoings but the question arises, whether they should be treated in the same way as adults or not. While some people believe that young offenders should face tougher punishments, others argue that they should be dealt with differently. In my opinion, young people who commit crimes should be treated according to their age, mental state, and the severity of their crime.

Firstly, age plays a key factor in how young people should be treated for committing crimes. Children and teenagers are emotionally and mentally immature compared to adults. They may make bad decisions and behave impulsively due to their lack of experience and knowledge. Therefore, it is necessary to take their age into consideration while treating them for their crimes. They can not be subjected to the same level of punishment as adults.

Secondly, young offenders' mental state should also be acknowledged. Many young people come from dysfunctional families or have been victims of abuse or trauma. Therefore, they may become involved in criminal activities as a result of their circumstances. Instead of treating them as adults, the authorities should work towards addressing the underlying problems and providing mental health assistance to help rehabilitate them.

Lastly, the severity of the crime committed by young people should also be considered before passing judgment on them. If the crime committed is minor, then it might be more appropriate to deal with the person concerned more gently. If, however, the crime is severe, such as murder or rape, then it is appropriate to treat the person as an adult.

In conclusion, young people who commit crimes should not be treated the same way as adults. Their age, mental state, and severity of the crime should be given due consideration before passing judgment. Rehabilitation an support should be prioritized over harsh punishments to help ensure that young people have a second chance to reform and become responsible citizens.
