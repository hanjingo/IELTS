The maps illustrate the changes in the layout of road access to a city hospital in 2007 and 2010.

Overall, the road access to the hospital underwent significant improvements, including the construction of a new roundabout, a bus station, and a separate car park for staff and the public.

In 2007, the city hospital was accessible via Hospital Road, which ran from the south to the north of the area. To the east of the hospital was the City Road, and to the west, there was a ring road surrounding the hospital. There were three bus stops located along Hospital Road, and a car park to the southeast of the hospital, shared by staff and the public.

By 2010, several key changes had been implemented. A roundabout was constructed at the intersection of Hospital Road and City Road, improving traffic flow. Another roundabout was built further north on Hospital Road, connecting it to the new bus station, which replaced the tree separate bus stops. The bus station was situated to the west of Hospital Road, adjacent to the hospital.

Additionally, the car park was divided into two separate areas. A new public car park was created to the east of the hospital, accessible via the ring road, while the original car park was designated for staff only.

These developments significantly enhanced the efficiency of road access to the city hospital, reducing congestion and providing clearer access routes for both staff and visitors.
