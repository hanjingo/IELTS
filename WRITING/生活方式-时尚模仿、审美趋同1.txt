Humans tend to copy one another, especially in fashion, choice of clothes and consumer goods. Do you agree or disagree. Provide your reasons and example from your own experience.

Nowadays fashion is spreading its roots in this jet age of the 21st century. It has been extremely debatable whether humans idolize to imitate others or not in terms of outfits, food items so on. Here, I would like to agree with the given statement.

There are numerous reasons to support my viewpoint. First and foremost, global advertisement has made the world as a global village and various fashion companies establish their branches in entire world to sale innovative fashion things in every supermarket of the world. As a result, people try to collect those things which are prevalent all over the world. For example, china sells its products frequently not only in India but also in other parts of the world as they are economically simultaneously superior. Consequently, people prefer to imitate others in fashion and consumer goods.

Furthermore, we all are living in a glamorous where various fashion industries develop new fashion all across the world further, people use them and try to imitate. Consequently, advertisements tempt people to wear the same dress as their celebrities. In addition, there is a dire need for everyone to update with the modern world trends therefore, people adopt new inventions of fashion in whole world.

What is more, in term of lifestyle, people want to compete with each others by uplifting their life as they want all things which are using by their neighbors and community. As a result, people adore to copy others in terms of consumer goods and fashion. Moreover, due to technology, people come to know about popular things that are in vogue worldwide therefore, they indulge in a race to achieve prevalent things through the internet so, they try to copy others.

To recapitulate, I think, it has become a fashion to imitate others to enhance their lifestyle.
