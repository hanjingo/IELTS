People living in 21st century have a better life quality than the people who lived in the previous centuries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

In the 21st century, life quality has improved immensely compared to the previous centuries. The advancements in technology, medicine, education, and transportation have made people's lives easier and more convenient. However, it cannot be denied that some aspects of life were better in the past. In this essay, I will discuss both sides of the argument and come up with a conclusion.

Firstly, it is undeniable that people today have a better life quality. With the advancements in technology, people have access to the internet, smartphones, computers, and other gadgets that make life easier. People can connect with their loved ones who live far away, work from home, shop online, and get any information they need within seconds. Moreover, medical advancements have helped to prolong life expectancy, treat previously deadly diseases, and improve the quality of life for people with medical conditions. Education is more accessible than ever, and people can now learn and get degrees from anywhere in the world. Transportation has also become faster and more comfortable, with airplanes, trains, and cars making commuting much easier.

On the other hand, life was simpler and less stressful in the past. People used to have more leisure time and were not as rushed as they are today. Moreover, people were more connected to nature and had a better understanding of the environment. People used to grow their food, build their houses, and make their clothes, which gave them a sense of satisfaction and pride. In addition, personal relationships used to be more valued, and people used to communicate face-to-face rather than through technology.

In conclusion, while it is true that people in the 21st century have a better life quality, it is also important to appreciate the value of the past. Personally, I would argue that a balance between the two would be ideal, where we enjoy the benefits of modernity while preserving the positive aspects of the past.
