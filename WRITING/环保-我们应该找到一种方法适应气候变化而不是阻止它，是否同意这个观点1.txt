Some people think that instead of preventing climate change, we need to find a way to live with it. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write an essay with no less than 250 words.

Climate change has become a global phenomenon, which has attracted the attention of people globally. There have been various debates on whether to prevent climate change or find a way to live with it. Whereas some believe that mitigating climate change should be a priority, others think of living with climate change as a better strategy. I strongly believe that we should focus on preventing climate change rather than living with it.

Preventing climate change can help maintain the natural balance of the environment. Climate change has caused a significant impact on the earth's natural systems, such as an increase in temperature, melting of polar ice, and severe weather conditions. As a result, many species of plants and animals have been heavily affected, and some are facing extinction. Focusing on preventing climate change can help minimize the negative effects of climate change on the planet and its inhabitants.

Moreover, relying on the strategy of living with climate change could prove costly in the long run. It could lead to unsustainable practices, which could pose a significant threat to the environment in the future. Although living with climate change might help cope with its impact for some time, it would not be a viable solution in the long term. Hence, it is essential to prevent climate change and its associated impacts.

In conclusion, climate change is a significant problem that has a damaging effect on the planet and its inhabitants. Although some people might argue that we should find a way to live with it, I believe that we should focus on preventing it. The effort invested now to mitigate the effects of climate change will go a long way in securing the future of the planet and its inhabitants.
